Welcome to KeepinVault ("KeepinVault", "the App", "we", "our", or "us").

KeepinVault is an offline-first Android application designed to help users securely create, organize, manage, and access their personal information directly on their own devices.

The App provides features for managing personal notes, vault items, chapters, reminders, imported files, images, audio recordings, and other user-created content while prioritizing local storage and user privacy.

2.1 Offline-first Philosophy

KeepinVault is designed as an offline-first Android application. Its primary purpose is to allow you to create, organize, and manage your Personal Content directly on your Device without relying on developer-operated servers.

Whenever possible, Personal Content is processed and stored locally to maximize privacy, improve reliability, and reduce unnecessary data transmission.

2.2 Privacy Principles

KeepinVault has been developed around the following privacy principles:

  • Your Personal Content belongs to you.
  • Your Personal Content is stored locally on your Device.
  • You remain in control of the information you create, import, manage, share, or delete.
  • The Developer does not collect or maintain developer-operated copies of your Personal Content during the normal operation of the App.
  • Privacy-related decisions are designed to favor local processing whenever reasonably possible.

Local Storage

KeepinVault is designed to store your Personal Content locally on your Device. During the normal operation of the App, Personal Content is not stored on developer-operated servers.

Depending on the type of information, KeepinVault stores metadata within its local database while storing file-based content within the App's internal storage.

5.1 Room Database

KeepinVault uses a local Room Database to organize and manage application information.

The Room Database primarily stores metadata and application records necessary for the App's operation, including references to locally stored content.

Examples may include:

  • Notes
  • Chapters
  • Vault items
  • Reminder information
  • Tags
  • Application preferences
  • References to imported files
  • References to images
  • References to audio recordings

The Room Database is stored locally on your Device.

5.2 Vault Storage

Vault items and their associated metadata are stored locally on your Device.

Where vault items include file-based content, the associated metadata is maintained within the Room Database while the actual files remain within the App's internal storage.

5.3 Imported Files

Supported files that you choose to import into KeepinVault are stored within the App's internal storage.

Information describing those files, such as their associated records or references required for application functionality, may be stored within the Room Database.

Imported files remain under your control and are not uploaded to developer-operated servers during the App's normal operation.

5.4 Images

Images created or imported through KeepinVault are stored within the App's internal storage.

Information required to organize or display those images may be stored within the Room Database.

5.5 Audio Recordings

Audio recordings created using KeepinVault are stored within the App's internal storage.

Associated information required for organization or application functionality may be maintained within the Room Database.

5.6 Reminder Data

Reminder information, scheduling details, and related metadata are stored locally on your Device using the App's local data storage.

This information is used only to provide reminder functionality within KeepinVault.

5.7 Settings

Application settings and user preferences are stored locally on your Device.

These settings help KeepinVault remember your preferences and application configuration.

5.8 Security Credentials

Configuration related to App Lock features, such as your selected authentication method and associated application settings, is stored locally on your Device.

Biometric templates, fingerprints, facial recognition data, and similar biometric information are not stored by KeepinVault. Biometric authentication is handled by the Android operating system.

5.9 Recycle Bin

Deleted Personal Content is initially moved to the Recycle Bin.

Items remain in the Recycle Bin until you permanently remove them.

While content remains in the Recycle Bin, it may be recoverable through the functionality provided by the App.

5.10 Cache

KeepinVault may create temporary cache files as part of its normal operation to improve performance and user experience.
